# Benta Business Control — User Guide

> Simple guide for **Super Admins**, **Owners**, and **Staff**.

---

## The app has 5 pages

| Tab | Name | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| 🏠 | **Home** | See how your business is doing right now |
| 💰 | **Sell** | Take a customer's order and collect payment |
| ➕ | **Record** | Enter restocks, expenses, production, or cash shifts |
| 📋 | **History** | See all money in and out in one timeline |
| ⚙️ | **Settings** | Account, offline, reports, and admin configuration |

---

## Signing in

1. Open the app in your browser.
2. Enter your email and password, tap **Sign in**.
3. You land on **Home** showing your branch's numbers.

> There is no sign-up form. An owner or super admin must invite you first from **Settings → Admin → Team**.

---

## Home — "How are we doing?"

At a glance you see:
- **Today's Sales** — total revenue
- **Today's Profit** — revenue minus food cost and expenses
- **Cash on Hand** — cash sales collected today
- **📈 Earning today** or **📉 Losing today** — instant health check
- **Money In vs Money Out** — simple breakdown
- **Alerts** — low stock items, pending approvals, open shifts
- **Recent sales** — last few transactions

Change the date or scope (branch / shop / all shops) using the controls at the top.

---

## Sell — Take an order

1. Tap products in the grid to add them to the cart.
2. Use **−** / **+** to adjust quantity.
3. **Pick the required flavors** for each item (e.g. 1 flavor for Solo meals, 2 for 6-piece).
4. Choose payment: **Cash** or **GCash**.
5. For Cash: type the amount received — change is calculated automatically.
6. Tap **Complete sale**.

If you're offline, the sale is saved locally and syncs when you reconnect.

## Record — Enter business activity

One page with 4 tabs:

### Restock tab
Record ingredient purchases. Example entries:
- Chicken wings — 15 kg at ₱2,200
- Cooking oil — 1 gallon at ₱350
- Rice — 25 kg at ₱1,100

Fill in: ingredient, quantity, cost per unit, supplier (optional), delivery fee (optional), and **date** (backdate for past purchases).

### Expense tab
Record any business cost. Example entries:
- Motor gas — ₱100
- Electricity — ₱3,500
- Packaging — ₱500

Fill in: category, amount, description, delivery fee (optional), and **date** (backdate for past expenses).

If paid from the cash drawer, check the toggle.

### Production tab
For batch-produced products only. Record when you cook/prepare a batch:
- Select product, enter actual yield, waste amount, and waste reason.
- Raw ingredients are consumed and finished goods are added.

### Cash tab
Manage your cash drawer:
- **Open shift** — enter opening cash amount.
- **Close shift** — count your cash, enter the amount. The system shows expected vs actual and over/short.
- Request **payouts** (took cash out for a purchase) or **safe drops** (moved cash to safe).

## History — See everything

One combined timeline showing ALL money movement:
- 💰 **Sales** — money coming in (green)
- 📦 **Restocks** — money going out for ingredients (red)
- 💸 **Expenses** — other costs (red)
- ↩️ **Corrections** — voids and refunds

Filter by type using the tabs at the top. Adjust the date range to see past activity.

**Summary cards** at the top show: Total Money In, Total Money Out, and Net.

Tap any sale to see its full detail (items, payment, cost).

## Settings — Configuration and reports

### Account tab (everyone)
- Your name, email, role, shop, and branch
- **Sign out** button

### Offline tab (everyone)
- Set up a PIN to encrypt offline sales
- Register this device for offline use
- See pending offline sales count

### Reports tab (owners and super admins)
See profit and loss over any date range:
- **Net revenue** — sales minus refunds
- **Net profit** — revenue minus food cost and expenses
- **Food cost %** — how much of revenue goes to ingredients
- **P&L breakdown** — gross profit, COGS, expenses, net profit
- **Top products** and **top flavors** by popularity
- **Cash and payment breakdown**

### Admin tab (owners and super admins)
All business configuration in one place:
- **Shops & branches** — create, rename, archive/restore (no delete)
- **Team** — add/remove staff, owners, super admins
- **Menu** — products, prices, flavor options, recipes
- **Ingredients** — define what you use (chicken, rice, oil, etc.)
- **Payments** — configure Cash, GCash, or custom methods
- **Settings** — tax configuration, receipt footer
- **Devices** — manage registered offline devices
- **Audit** — see who changed what and when

## Roles

| What | Staff | Owner | Super Admin |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| See Home dashboard | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Take sales | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Record restocks, expenses, production | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Open/close cash shifts | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Request void/refund | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| View History | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Approve voids, refunds, cash requests | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| View Reports | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Manage menu, recipes, ingredients | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Manage team, shops, branches | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Create new shops | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |

**Staff** — assigned to a specific branch. Can only operate there.
**Owners** — assigned to a shop. Can see all branches in that shop.
**Super Admins** — see everything in the business group.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Branch** | A physical location or counter |
| **Shop** | A business concept (chicken wings, takoyaki, etc.) |
| **COGS** | Cost of goods sold — ingredient cost consumed by sales |
| **Made to order** | Sale directly uses raw ingredients |
| **Batch produced** | Cook first, then sell from prepared stock |
| **Cash shift** | Period from opening cash to closing count |
| **Payout** | Cash taken from drawer for a purchase |
| **Safe drop** | Cash moved from drawer to safe |
